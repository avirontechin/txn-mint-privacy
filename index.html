TxnMint Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026

1. Overview

TxnMint is a personal expense tracking application provided by AvironTech. This policy describes the information processed by the Android app and how it is used.

2. Financial information

Transactions you enter, including amounts, dates, categories, merchant details and notes, are stored locally on your device using the app’s local storage system. TxnMint does not provide a cloud account or transaction synchronization service in the reviewed implementation.

3. Optional SMS features

The current release configuration excludes restricted SMS-reading and SMS-receiving permissions. SMS-related functionality exists in development configurations but is not available in the Play Store release configuration reviewed for this policy. If a future release enables SMS access, this policy and the app’s disclosures will be updated before that release.

4. Analytics and crash reporting

When Firebase is configured in a production build, Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics are enabled. These services may collect app interaction information, device and app identifiers, device characteristics, crash logs and diagnostic information to help us understand usage and fix problems. Firebase Remote Config may also communicate with Google services to retrieve app configuration. These data may be processed by Google as our service provider. The reviewed configuration disables advertising ID collection and ad-personalization signals.

5. Purposes and sharing

We use locally stored financial information to provide expense tracking, budgets, transaction history and spending analysis. We use Firebase information for app analytics, diagnostics and configuration. We do not use TxnMint to offer loans, transfer money or sell financial products. Information processed by Firebase is shared with Google as a technology service provider. We do not claim that all information remains on your device.

6. Data storage and security

Financial records are designed to be stored in encrypted local application storage. Android backup is disabled in the reviewed configuration. We apply technical safeguards, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention and deletion

You can delete locally stored financial records using the app’s data-management deletion feature. This also clears associated local encryption material and withdraws SMS consent as described by the current implementation. Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics data are subject to Google Firebase retention and deletion controls; deleting local app data does not necessarily delete records already processed by Google. Contact us for privacy-related requests.

8. Your choices

You can choose what financial transactions to enter, manage relevant Android permissions in device settings, and use the app’s local data-deletion controls. You may contact us with questions about data handling.

9. Children

TxnMint is a personal finance management application and is not specifically directed to children.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as the app or its data practices change. The current version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
